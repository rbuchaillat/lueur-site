Privacy policy

Updated November 12, 2026

In short: Lueur collects no data. Your phrases, favorites and settings stay on your phone.

Who we are

Lueur is published by Buchaillat Corp. For any question about this policy: remibuchaillat.pro@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your phone

Your themes, favorites, the phrases you write, the phrases you have read, the ones you removed and your settings are saved in a file on your phone, in a space shared with Lueur’s widgets. There is no account and no server: none of it leaves your phone and we have no access to it.

Your background photo

With Lueur Pro you can use a photo as a background. The system photo picker gives Lueur only the photo you choose, not your library. A smaller copy is kept on your phone for the app and widgets. It is never uploaded.

Shared images

When you share a phrase as an image, the system share sheet sends it to whoever you choose. To save it to your photos, Lueur only asks for permission to add an image, not to read your library.

Backup

“Export” creates a file with your phrases and favorites that you keep wherever you like (Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive…). The file stays under your control: we never receive a copy.

Read aloud

Reading aloud uses your phone’s built-in speech voice. The text is not sent to any service.

Reminders

Reminders are notifications scheduled by your phone itself, with no server. You can turn them off in the app’s Settings.

Lueur Pro purchase

Lueur Pro is unlocked with a one-time purchase on the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google: we receive no name, email or payment information. To know whether Pro is unlocked, the app asks the store, which answers yes or no. This is the app’s only exchange with the internet.

When you write to us

“Contact support” and “Report a mistake” open your email app. If you send the message, we receive your email address and what you wrote (for a mistake, the phrase number). We use it only to answer you and fix the phrase, and we delete the message if you ask.

What we don’t collect

  • No personal data (name, email, phone, address, location), unless you write to us.
  • None of your phrases, favorites, history or photos.
  • No usage statistics, no analytics, no ads, no trackers.
  • No third-party services inside the app.

Crash reports

If you agreed in your phone’s settings to share analytics with developers, Apple or Google may send us technical crash reports. They don’t contain your phrases.

Deletion

Deleting the app erases everything it saved. Since we have no copy, there is nothing to ask us to delete on our side.

Children

Lueur collects no data, including from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its date. If the app ever had to send data off your phone, this page would say so first.
